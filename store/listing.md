## Short description (132 characters max)

Pixel-art new tab with widgets, spaces and voices that roast, cling to or mourn your to-do list. No account, no tracking.

## Detailed description

Your new tab, with a personality problem.

Startpage is a pixel-art new tab page with the widgets you'd expect: search with commands and math, clock, weather and forecast, tasks, notes, bookmarks, a focus timer, habits with streaks, calendar, countdowns, world clocks, GitHub activity and news. You can group them into spaces such as Home, Work, Gaming and Dev.

Then pick a voice, and the entire page, settings included, is rewritten in that voice's words:

• Monday: "Ugh. You again." Rude, tired, and honest.
• Super Supportive: never leaving you. Counts your visits. Kept a copy of that task you deleted.
• Surfer: cannot remember your name. "Chr… Chris… Christo… dude."
• Professional: documenting you for a performance review.
• Therapist: bills by the minute.
• Weepy: mourns every task you finish.
• Victorian: disapproves of you, the internet, and tabs.
• …and three secret voices you have to earn.

The voices react as things happen: finishing a task, pausing the timer at 23:41, breaking a streak, opening a tab at 3am. Now and then they interrupt each other. Every Sunday you get a weekly recap narrated by your voice, which you can save as an image. Bring your own Claude API key and you can ask your voice anything.

Private by design: no account, no analytics, no server. Your data stays in your browser. Optional sync goes through a private GitHub Gist in your own account.

## Privacy practices (dashboard answers)

- Single purpose: replaces the new tab page with a customizable dashboard.
- Permissions: none requested.
- Remote code: none. All code ships in the package.
- Data usage: the extension does not collect or transmit user data to the developer. Requests go directly from the user's browser to the third-party services listed in PRIVACY.md, and only when the user enables them.
- Privacy policy URL: https://github.com/vardirhq/startpage/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
